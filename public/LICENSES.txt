Dialogue portraits
------------------
public/portraits/logan.png and public/portraits/angie.png are derived from the approved character
boards (docs/art/logan-approved-01, docs/art/angie-approved-01), which were developed from
Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) layers. They are distributed under CC-BY-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Changes: layer composition, recolouring,
photo-guided editing of hair, face and proportions, extraction to pixel scale, Logan's shirt
recolouring, Angie's veil added. Source repository: https://github.com/sanderfrenken/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator at commit 675e21e04aaff8486a3a24e09573b3d5af9d28b9.
